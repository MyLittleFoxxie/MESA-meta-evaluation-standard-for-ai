# References review, November 2026

Written on 11 November 2026 in round 1 of the REFSQ review rounds. It covers `references.bib` and the citations in `mesa_paper.tex` and `paper2.tex`.

How entries were checked: each cited entry was compared with its source (authors, title, venue, year, DOI) where the source could be opened from this session. The column "Source opened" says which page was read. "Not fetched" means the entry was not compared with a source in this round; those are older works whose records were not opened, and they are listed so that you can check them before submission. Web pages were read through a fetch tool that returns a summary, so a detail such as a middle initial can still differ.

## Limits of this review

- The searches for missing work were a small number of web searches on the topics in the plan, not a systematic review. `literature/slr-2026-10-28/` and `literature/slr-extension-2026-11/` hold the systematic searches.
- Entries marked "Not fetched" or "Not opened" were not compared with a source in this round.
- REFSQ 2027 may check papers for references that do not exist, and many such references are a ground for desk rejection. Every entry cited by either paper that could be opened does exist at its source.
