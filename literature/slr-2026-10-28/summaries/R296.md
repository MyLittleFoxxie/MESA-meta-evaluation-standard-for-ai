# R296: Do Large Language Model Benchmarks Test Reliability?

Vendrow, Joshua; Vendrow, Edward; Beery, Sara; Madry, Aleksander. First public date recorded: 2025-02-05.

[Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.03461) · [Inspected source](<../fulltext/R296.pdf>)

**Source type.** Benchmark audit and curated evaluation

**Purpose.** Examine errors hidden by aggregate benchmark scores.

**Intended reviewers.** Benchmark researchers and model evaluators.

**Concepts and criteria.** Reliability as consistently correct performance on curated tasks; annotation correctness.

**Answer format and guidance.** Curated platinum subsets and error analysis.

**Missing information.** Unexamined items may retain errors; agreement across models does not establish a correct reference answer.

**Accessibility and effort.** No reader accessibility test.

**Evaluation and findings.** Builds 15 curated benchmark subsets and examines model errors even on tasks expected to be straightforward.

**Evidence assessment.** Small subsets and selection around observed errors can miss shared failures; reliability here differs from OECD test retest variance.

**Evidence locator.** Sections 2–5 and appendices; limitations section.
