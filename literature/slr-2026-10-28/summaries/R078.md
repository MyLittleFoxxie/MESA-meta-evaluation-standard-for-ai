# R078: Assessment Design in the AI Era: A Method for Identifying Items Functioning Differentially for Humans and Chatbots

Zeinfeld, Licol; Strugatski, Alona; Bar-Dov, Ziva; Blonder, Ron; Rap, Shelley; Alexandron, Giora. First public date recorded: 2026-03-24.

[Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23682) · [Inspected source](<../fulltext/R078.pdf>)

**Source type.** Empirical measurement method

**Purpose.** Identify assessment items that behave differently for humans and chatbots.

**Intended reviewers.** Assessment designers with psychometric expertise.

**Concepts and criteria.** Differential item functioning; item total relationships; negative controls.

**Answer format and guidance.** Statistical diagnostics rather than reviewer categorical judgments.

**Missing information.** Test design and diagnostic evidence are separate from missing documentation. No review missingness scale.

**Accessibility and effort.** No review comprehension or effort evaluation in the inspected experiment.

**Evaluation and findings.** Applies diagnostics to two tests and six chatbots, examining item functioning and human comparisons.

**Evidence assessment.** Specific tests and models constrain transfer; model scores are not human reviewer responses.

**Evidence locator.** Sections 3–5, PDF pp. 3–6.
