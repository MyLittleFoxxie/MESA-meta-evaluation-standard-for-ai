# B06: HiBayES: A Hierarchical Bayesian Modeling Framework for AI Evaluation Statistics

Luettgau, Lennart; Coppock, Harry; Dubois, Magda; Summerfield, Christopher; Ududec, Cozmin. First public date recorded: 2025-05-08.

[Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.05602) · [Inspected source](<../fulltext/B06.pdf>)

**Source type.** Statistical framework

**Purpose.** Estimate capabilities and uncertainty for nested evaluations.

**Intended reviewers.** Researchers with statistical/modeling expertise.

**Concepts and criteria.** Hierarchical Bayesian GLMs, partial pooling and model comparison.

**Answer format and guidance.** Worked examples and software package.

**Missing information.** Uncertainty is modeled rather than replaced by a point score; assumptions remain part of the evidence.

**Accessibility and effort.** Implementation guidance; no novice reviewer study.

**Evaluation and findings.** Illustrative applications and method comparisons demonstrate statistical inference.

**Evidence assessment.** Results depend on priors, hierarchy and model fit; not a validation of benchmark construct meaning.

**Evidence locator.** Sections 2–5 and examples; 23 page PDF.
