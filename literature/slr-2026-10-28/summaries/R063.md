# R063: SIGHT: A Methodological Appraisal Framework for Evaluating Large Language Models in Ophthalmology

Vahedi, Hadi; Razmjooei, Fatemeh; Semnani, Farbod; Vahedi, Parviz; Alizadehsani, Roohallah; Jafarizadeh, Ali. First public date recorded: 2026-10-11.

[Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15958) · [Inspected source](<../fulltext/R063.pdf>)

**Source type.** Appraisal framework and literature synthesis

**Purpose.** Review methods used to evaluate LLMs in ophthalmology.

**Intended reviewers.** Researchers, journal reviewers and evidence reviewers in medicine.

**Concepts and criteria.** 20 items across Sample, Independence, Ground truth, Handling repeated/paired data and Transparency.

**Answer format and guidance.** Yes/Partial/No or NR with 2/1/0 points and NA; item explanations and applicability rules.

**Missing information.** No and not reported share a scoring category. Record this loss of distinction when comparing answer formats.

**Accessibility and effort.** Detailed anchors can guide review, but length and anchors are not demonstrated accessibility.

**Evaluation and findings.** Synthesises ophthalmology studies and proposes SIGHT. Limitations section describes the framework as requiring further validation.

**Evidence assessment.** Clinical scope, literature selection and unvalidated appraisal thresholds limit transfer to a general form.

**Evidence locator.** Methods PDF p. 7; framework pp. 34–51; limitations pp. 52–53.
