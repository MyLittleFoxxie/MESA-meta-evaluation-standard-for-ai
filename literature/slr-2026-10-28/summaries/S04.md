# S04: Standards for Educational and Psychological Testing

American Educational Research Association; American Psychological Association; National Council on Measurement in Education. First public date recorded: 2014.

[Primary record](https://www.testingstandards.net/uploads/7/6/6/4/76643089/standards_2014edition.pdf) · [Inspected source](<../fulltext/S04.pdf>)

**Source type.** Foundation standard (2014 exception)

**Purpose.** Set standards for evidence supporting test score interpretations and uses.

**Intended reviewers.** Test developers, users, policymakers and measurement professionals.

**Concepts and criteria.** Validity; reliability/precision; fairness; design, administration, reporting, documentation and use.

**Answer format and guidance.** Standards with explanatory comments rather than a short checklist or one quality score.

**Missing information.** Calls for evidence and disclosure of limitations; lack of supporting evidence limits interpretation. Does not prescribe MESA response choices.

**Accessibility and effort.** Fair access and test taker rights concern assessment participants. They do not demonstrate accessibility of a benchmark review procedure.

**Evaluation and findings.** Professional standards; not an experiment or an AI benchmark audit.

**Evidence assessment.** Authority and applicability assessed separately from empirical findings. Human test concepts require explicit transfer arguments when applied to AI.

**Evidence locator.** Chapters 1–3, printed pp. 11–72; chapters 6–7, pp. 111–130; introductory scope.

**Version.** Retrieved edition/version: see source file and version audit. Earlier public versions not fully compared.
