**Source type.** Reporting framework, interviews and corpus audit

**Purpose.** Present evaluation information for different readers.

**Intended reviewers.** Technical evaluators, AI engineers, policy actors and general readers.

**Concepts and criteria.** Provenance, reproducibility, reporting completeness and comparability.

**Answer format and guidance.** Structured evaluation cards; summary and research modes; plain language caveats and missing field signals.

**Missing information.** Explicit missing reporting signals; documentation completeness is different from the validity of a claim.

**Accessibility and effort.** Stakeholder interviews and feedback support design. Quotes about time savings are perceptions, not timed task results; systematic usability work is planned.

**Evaluation and findings.** Same PDF reports 10 interviews/101,843 results in abstract and 12/101,955 in body. Use neither pair as a settled denominator without resolving the conflict.

**Evidence assessment.** Strong counterexample to claims that no audience tailored interpretation tools exist. Corpus counts and interview denominators require author confirmation.

**Evidence locator.** Sections 3–5, especially 4.3 PDF pp. 7–8; appendix B; version-audit.md.
