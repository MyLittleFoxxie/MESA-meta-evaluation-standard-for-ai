# S07: BetterBench: Assessing AI Benchmarks, Uncovering Issues, and Establishing Best Practices

Reuel, Anka; Hardy, Amelia; Smith, Chandler; Lamparth, Max; Hardy, Malcolm; Kochenderfer, Mykel J.. First public date recorded: 2024-11-20.

[Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.12990) · [Inspected source](<../fulltext/S07-proceedings.pdf>)

**Source type.** Review framework with benchmark audit

**Purpose.** Assess benchmark construction, documentation, implementation and maintenance.

**Intended reviewers.** Benchmark developers, evaluators and model users.

**Concepts and criteria.** 46 criteria across development and application in the inspected PDFs; documentation, validity, reliability, reproducibility and usability of benchmarks.

**Answer format and guidance.** Criterion specific rubrics using 0/5/10/15; examples and guidance; stakeholder interviews and assessment calibration.

**Missing information.** Missing documentation can receive zero under particular rubrics. That documents evidence availability and does not establish every underlying practice was absent.

**Accessibility and effort.** Benchmark usability includes implementation and documentation access. Stakeholder input is evidence of design consultation; it is not a controlled novice comprehension or time study.

**Evaluation and findings.** Inspected local and proceedings PDFs report assessment of 24 benchmarks using 46 criteria. The proceedings landing abstract instead reports 25 benchmarks and 40 practices; retain this conflict.

**Evidence assessment.** Rubrics and author assessments support an audit, not proof of accessibility gains. Do not mix the landing abstract counts with the inspected PDF methods.

**Evidence locator.** Sections 3–5; appendices with rubrics and interview questions; version-audit.md.
