# S03: EFPA Test Review Model, Version 2025

European Federation of Psychologists’ Associations. First public date recorded: 2025-08-25.

[Primary record](https://www.efpa.eu/resource/efpa-test-review-model-version-2025/) · [Inspected source](<../../pdfs/EFPA_test_review_model_2025.pdf>)

**Source type.** Professional review model

**Purpose.** Guide description and evaluation of psychological and educational tests.

**Intended reviewers.** Reviewers with expertise in psychological testing; national associations implementing test reviews.

**Concepts and criteria.** Part A description; Part B rationale/documentation, materials, norms, reliability, validity, fair use and generated reports; final evaluation.

**Answer format and guidance.** Forms with explanatory guidance and item dependent anchors; n/a and 0–4 ratings. Overall judgments require interpretation, not a mechanical average.

**Missing information.** Separates n/a and absent evidence from inadequacy: for example section 6.4 has 0 = no information, 1 = inadequate. Read each anchor in context.

**Accessibility and effort.** Definitions and guidance support trained reviewers. Test taker accessibility criteria are not evidence that the review model is accessible to novice reviewers.

**Evaluation and findings.** Normative guidance drawing on prior test review practice. The 2025 model does not report an AI benchmark reviewer trial in the inspected text.

**Evidence assessment.** Assess authority, scope and transfer assumptions. Adaptation to AI is a research decision, not EFPA endorsement. The complete 90 page source includes forms and guidance.

**Evidence locator.** Introduction PDF pp. 6–8; Parts A/B; section 6.4 PDF30, fair use sections; Part C PDF86–89.

**Version.** Retrieved edition/version: see source file and version audit. Earlier public versions not fully compared.
