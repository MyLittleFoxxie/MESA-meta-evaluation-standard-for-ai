# B15: Challenges in Trustworthy Human Evaluation of Chatbots

Zhao, Wenting; Rush, Alexander M.; Goyal, Tanya. First public date recorded: 2024-12-05.

[Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.04363) · [Inspected source](<../fulltext/B15.pdf>)

**Source type.** Empirical annotation sensitivity study

**Purpose.** Examine effects of unreliable preference votes.

**Intended reviewers.** AI evaluation researchers; technical knowledge is assumed by the method.

**Concepts and criteria.** Apathetic, adversarial and variable judgments in open leaderboards.

**Answer format and guidance.** Method, definitions or recommendations as described in the evaluation field; no general categorical benchmark review questionnaire.

**Missing information.** No shared missing information response scale. Report unavailable evidence separately from the observed experimental finding.

**Accessibility and effort.** No measured novice benchmark review understanding or effort identified in the inspected method/results. This is not a claim about every possible use of the work.

**Evaluation and findings.** Simulation and analysis of poor quality votes; 10 percent can move rankings by up to five places in studied conditions.

**Evidence assessment.** The simulated votes are not an estimate of actual bad vote prevalence; disagreement can reflect legitimate preferences.

**Evidence locator.** Section 3 analyses; conclusion 4 and limitations PDF pp. 5–6.
