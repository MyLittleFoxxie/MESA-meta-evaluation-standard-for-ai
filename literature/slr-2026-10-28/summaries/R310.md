**Source type.** Survey

**Purpose.** Explain benchmark contamination detection and mitigation.

**Intended reviewers.** LLM evaluators with technical expertise.

**Concepts and criteria.** Matching, comparison, contamination effects and benchmark alternatives.

**Evidence assessment.** Detector performance depends on access and contamination definition.

**Evidence locator.** Sections 2–5, especially detection sections 3.1–3.2 and mitigation section 4.
