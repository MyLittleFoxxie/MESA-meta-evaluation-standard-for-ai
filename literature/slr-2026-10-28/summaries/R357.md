# R357: Reliability of the QUADAS-2 checklist in AI-based medical imaging: A comparison of human reviewers and ChatGPT-4o

Mahmood Dashti; Farshad Khosraviani; Niloofar Ghadimi; Saman Kalantari; Mohammad Hosein Amirzade-Iranaq; Ghazal Hemmati; Sarah Arzani; Zeynab Pirayesh; Parisa Motie. First public date recorded: 2026-01-01.

[Primary record](https://doi.org/10.5624/isd.20250325) · [Inspected source](<../fulltext/R357.html>)

**Source type.** Empirical reviewer study

**Purpose.** Evaluate agreement when applying QUADAS-2 to AI imaging studies.

**Intended reviewers.** Five dental reviewers with prior QUADAS-2 experience; repeated ChatGPT-4o runs.

**Concepts and criteria.** Risk of bias and applicability; categorical agreement.

**Answer format and guidance.** Low/high/unclear risk at seven domain judgments per study.

**Missing information.** Unclear is distinct from high risk. Agreement does not establish correct judgments.

**Accessibility and effort.** Evidence of actual reviewer use, but not novice accessibility or measured time savings.

**Evaluation and findings.** 20 studies, five human reviewers and five runs of one model. Human kappa ranged −0.07 to 0.22; model values −0.17 to 0.56. No calibration; pragmatic sampling.

**Evidence assessment.** Model runs are not independent people. Category prevalence affects kappa. Authors note sample, lack of calibration and unrecorded exact model session dates.

**Evidence locator.** HTML Materials and Methods, Results and Discussion.

**Version.** Retrieved edition/version: see source file and version audit. Earlier public versions not fully compared.
