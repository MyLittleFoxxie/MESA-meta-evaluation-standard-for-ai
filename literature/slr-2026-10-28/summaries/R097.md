# R097: Scorecard of AI Benchmark Quality

Ayrton San Joaquin; Rokas Gipiškis; Ze Shen Chin. First public date recorded: 2026.

[Primary record](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2026.evaleval-1.25) · [Inspected source](<../fulltext/R097.pdf>)

**Source type.** Scorecard with case applications

**Purpose.** Judge benchmark suitability for different uses.

**Intended reviewers.** Developers and third party assessors with varying backgrounds.

**Concepts and criteria.** Construct, internal and external validity, reliability and correctness.

**Answer format and guidance.** Satisfied/unsatisfied/NA with reasons; dimension scores 1–4 and suitability categories.

**Missing information.** Vague or absent documentation can count as nonfulfilment. This differs from preserving an unknown response.

**Accessibility and effort.** Guidance and explanations exist. Two assessors disagreed on six items for Vending-Bench 2; no measured novice comprehension or time.

**Evaluation and findings.** Case applications include ImpossibleBench and Vending-Bench 2; disagreement concerned item scope and evidential weight. Thresholds explicitly not empirically validated.

**Evidence assessment.** Direct counterexample to absence of review tools or reviewer evidence. Small assessor study cannot establish use across expertise levels.

**Evidence locator.** Sections 3–7, printed pp. 130–135; appendices A–D.

**Version.** Retrieved edition/version: see source file and version audit. Earlier public versions not fully compared.
