# S02: The Leaderboard Illusion

Singh, Shivalika; Nan, Yiyang; Wang, Alex; D'Souza, Daniel; Kapoor, Sayash; Üstün, Ahmet; Koyejo, Sanmi; Deng, Yuntian; Longpre, Shayne; Smith, Noah A.; Ermis, Beyza; Fadaee, Marzieh; Hooker, Sara. First public date recorded: 2025-04-29.

[Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.20879) · [Inspected source](<../../pdfs/The Leaderboard Illusion.pdf>)

**Source type.** Empirical platform audit

**Purpose.** Examine fairness and reliability of Chatbot Arena rankings.

**Intended reviewers.** Evaluation researchers, platform operators and users interpreting rankings.

**Concepts and criteria.** Private testing, selective reporting, sampling, data access, contamination and unequal provider participation.

**Answer format and guidance.** Analyses of platform practices followed by recommendations for disclosure, limits and sampling. No general review answer form.

**Missing information.** Observed private tests and publication choices are different from claims about undisclosed tests. Inference is constrained by accessible platform data.

**Accessibility and effort.** Explains how ranking practices affect interpretation; does not evaluate a reader training or questionnaire intervention.

**Evaluation and findings.** Audits private variants and provider participation, including 27 Meta variants, and studies effects on reported rankings. Findings concern the observed Arena setting.

**Evidence assessment.** Platform specific, time dependent observational evidence. Recommendations do not show that proposed changes improve novice judgments.

**Evidence locator.** Sections 3–9, PDF pp. 5–31; appendices document data and analyses.
