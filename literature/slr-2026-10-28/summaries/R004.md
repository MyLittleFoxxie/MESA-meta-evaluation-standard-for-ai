# R004: Benchmarking AI Models in Software Engineering: A Review, Search Tool, and Unified Approach for Elevating Benchmark Quality

Koohestani, Roham; de Bekker, Philippe; Koç, Begüm; Izadi, Maliheh. First public date recorded: 2025-03-07.

[Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.05860) · [Inspected source](<../fulltext/R004.pdf>)

**Source type.** Review, tool and empirical applications

**Purpose.** Organise software engineering benchmarks and improve selection and construction.

**Intended reviewers.** AI for software engineering researchers and practitioners.

**Concepts and criteria.** BenchScout search; BenchFrame quality guidance; benchmark repair and testing.

**Answer format and guidance.** Search interface, workflow and framework rather than a general categorical form.

**Missing information.** Documents benchmark limitations and repair decisions; no dedicated unknown versus no response format established here.

**Accessibility and effort.** 22 participants rated tool usability, effectiveness and intuitiveness. Their mean familiarity was 3.8/5; 15/22 rated familiarity 4 or 5. This is a counterexample to no human evidence, but not a sample of beginners.

**Evaluation and findings.** Reviews 247 studies and 273 benchmarks. User ratings 4.5, 4.0 and 4.1/5 are perceptions. Benchmark repair experiments concern changed benchmark outputs, not reviewer understanding.

**Evidence assessment.** Convenience sample, self reports and no controlled timed comparison. Separate the tool study from model evaluation of repaired benchmarks.

**Evidence locator.** Sections IV-C and VII-E; PDF pp. 8–10 and threats to validity section.
