# B11: State of What Art? A Call for Multi-Prompt LLM Evaluation

Mizrahi, Moran; Kaplan, Guy; Malkin, Dan; Dror, Rotem; Shahaf, Dafna; Stanovsky, Gabriel. First public date recorded: 2023-12-31.

[Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.00595) · [Inspected source](<../fulltext/B11.pdf>)

**Source type.** Empirical sensitivity study

**Purpose.** Test dependence of evaluations on instruction wording.

**Intended reviewers.** AI evaluation researchers; technical knowledge is assumed by the method.

**Concepts and criteria.** Prompt variation; absolute performance and model ranking.

**Answer format and guidance.** Method, definitions or recommendations as described in the evaluation field; no general categorical benchmark review questionnaire.

**Missing information.** No shared missing information response scale. Report unavailable evidence separately from the observed experimental finding.

**Accessibility and effort.** No measured novice benchmark review understanding or effort identified in the inspected method/results. This is not a claim about every possible use of the work.

**Evaluation and findings.** Experiments with 20 models, 39 tasks and 6.5 million instances; multi prompt metrics.

**Evidence assessment.** Sampling of paraphrases and tasks limits generality; best prompt and average prompt answer different questions.

**Evidence locator.** Sections 3–7 and conclusion 9, PDF pp. 3–12.
