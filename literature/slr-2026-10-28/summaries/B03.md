# B03: ECBD: Evidence-Centered Benchmark Design for NLP

Yu Lu Liu; Su Lin Blodgett; Jackie Chi Kit Cheung; Q. Vera Liao; Alexandra Olteanu; Ziang Xiao. First public date recorded: 2024-06-13.

[Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.08723) · [Inspected source](<../fulltext/B03.pdf>)

**Source type.** Design framework and case studies

**Purpose.** Make benchmark design choices and their evidential support explicit.

**Intended reviewers.** NLP benchmark designers and analysts.

**Concepts and criteria.** Five modules: capability, task, data, assembly and evidence; describe, justify and support.

**Answer format and guidance.** 20 question worksheet with guidance; applied to BoolQ, SuperGLUE and HELM.

**Missing information.** Separates describing a choice from justifying it and supplying evidence. Paper only analysis cannot establish absence from websites or code.

**Accessibility and effort.** A concise worksheet already exists. User studies of understanding and utility are explicitly future work.

**Evaluation and findings.** Three author case analyses identify description without adequate justification or validity evidence.

**Evidence assessment.** Only three benchmarks and their papers; author interpretation; validity scope omits other concerns such as privacy and reliability.

**Evidence locator.** Sections 3–5, worksheet appendix; Limitations printed pp. 16357–16358.
