# R257: Can We Trust AI Benchmarks? An Interdisciplinary Review of Current Issues in AI Evaluation

Maria Eriksson; Erasmo Purificato; Arman Noroozian; João Vinagre; Guillaume M. J. -B. Chaslot; Emilia Gómez; David Fernández Llorca. First public date recorded: 2025-10-15.

[Primary record](https://doi.org/10.1609/aies.v8i1.36595) · [Inspected source](<../fulltext/R257-fallback.pdf>)

**Source type.** Review

**Purpose.** Synthesize concerns about AI benchmarking.

**Intended reviewers.** Researchers, policymakers and evaluators.

**Concepts and criteria.** Nine issue areas spanning data, design, use and social incentives; validity and reporting.

**Answer format and guidance.** Narrative synthesis and recommendations, not one scored reviewer form.

**Missing information.** Distinguishes unknowns and reporting gaps from empirical demonstrations across underlying studies.

**Accessibility and effort.** No direct evaluation of novice reviewer use in this review.

**Evaluation and findings.** Meta review of about 110 studies over ten years. Its older evidence is not separately counted as included recent studies in this SLR.

**Evidence assessment.** A review is a map of claims, not an independent replication of each underlying experiment.

**Evidence locator.** Introduction, review method, issue sections and conclusion; AIES 2025 PDF pp. 1–15.

**Version.** Retrieved edition/version: see source file and version audit. Earlier public versions not fully compared.
