# S06: A Definition of AGI

Hendrycks, Dan; Song, Dawn; Szegedy, Christian; Lee, Honglak; Gal, Yarin; Brynjolfsson, Erik; Li, Sharon; Zou, Andy; Levine, Lionel; Han, Bo; Fu, Jie; Liu, Ziwei; Shin, Jinwoo; Lee, Kimin; Mazeika, Mantas; Phan, Long; Ingebretsen, George; Khoja, Adam; Xie, Cihang; Salaudeen, Olawale; Hein, Matthias; Zhao, Kevin; Pan, Alexander; Duvenaud, David; Li, Bo; Omohundro, Steve; Alfour, Gabriel; Tegmark, Max; McGrew, Kevin; Marcus, Gary; Tallinn, Jaan; Schmidt, Eric; Bengio, Yoshua. First public date recorded: 2025-10-21.

[Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.18212) · [Inspected source](<../../pdfs/A_Definition_of_AGI.pdf>)

**Source type.** Conceptual proposal with model application

**Purpose.** Define AGI through a profile of cognitive abilities and propose tests.

**Intended reviewers.** AI researchers and readers interpreting broad intelligence claims; assumes familiarity with cognitive assessment.

**Concepts and criteria.** CHC inspired ten domain account; distinguishes broad ability from task performance. Operational thresholds and aggregate AGI score are authors’ proposal.

**Answer format and guidance.** Definitions, testing notes, examples and domain thresholds. Not a general benchmark review checklist.

**Missing information.** Insufficient coverage cannot justify a broad intelligence claim. This work does not supply MESA style missing evidence categories.

**Accessibility and effort.** Examples explain domains; no measured accessibility of benchmark reviews in inspected sections.

**Evaluation and findings.** Applies a proposed scoring framework to AI systems. Demonstrates the proposal, not consensus on a definition or validation of every task/domain mapping.

**Evidence assessment.** Assess justification for domains, weights, thresholds and transfer from human cognition. Do not import its aggregate AGI score as benchmark quality.

**Evidence locator.** Sections 2–4 and appendices A–J; local PDF version v3, 57 pages; first public release 21 October 2025.
