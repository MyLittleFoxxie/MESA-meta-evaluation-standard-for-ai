**Source type.** Empirical audit and recommendations

**Purpose.** Examine whether benchmark evidence supports capability claims.

**Intended reviewers.** Benchmark researchers; annotation performed by experts.

**Concepts and criteria.** Phenomena, tasks, metrics, claims; content, convergent, discriminant and ecological validity.

**Answer format and guidance.** Annotation scheme and eight recommendations with a checklist; asks for explicit links between claims and evidence.

**Missing information.** Absence of a documented justification is an observation about reporting; it does not establish that no relevant capability exists.

**Accessibility and effort.** Explanations and checklist aid review. No measured novice understanding or review duration in the inspected method/results.

**Evaluation and findings.** 29 expert reviewers examined 445 benchmarks. The audit finds gaps in construct definitions and validity evidence. These are observations in the sampled papers, not a trial of reviewer accessibility.

**Evidence assessment.** Assess sampling, annotation criteria and expert judgments; benchmark selection and interpretive coding constrain generalization. No MESA comparison.

**Evidence locator.** Sections 2–5; annotation appendices; PDF pp. 1–11 and 16–29.
