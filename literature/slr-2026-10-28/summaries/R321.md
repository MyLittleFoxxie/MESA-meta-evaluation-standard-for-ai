# R321: Aligning Language Model Benchmarks with Pairwise Preferences

Gutierrez, Marco; Leng, Xinyi; Cyberey, Hannah; Schwarz, Jonathan Richard; Alaa, Ahmed; Hartvigsen, Thomas. First public date recorded: 2026-02-02.

[Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02898) · [Inspected source](<../fulltext/R321.pdf>)

**Source type.** Empirical statistical method

**Purpose.** Improve correspondence between benchmark scores and pairwise preferences.

**Intended reviewers.** Evaluation researchers with statistical expertise.

**Concepts and criteria.** Item weighting and preference alignment.

**Answer format and guidance.** Learned weights and comparisons, not a human review form.

**Missing information.** Unobserved preferences and distribution changes limit inference.

**Accessibility and effort.** No demonstrated novice review accessibility.

**Evaluation and findings.** Analyses 4,576 models across six benchmarks with preference based reweighting.

**Evidence assessment.** Improved correspondence on available data is not universal validity or correctness of human preferences.

**Evidence locator.** Section 4 and limitations section 5, PDF pp. 5–10.
