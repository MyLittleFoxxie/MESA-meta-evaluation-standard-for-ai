# R042: Fixing FOLIO and MALLS: Verified Annotations and an LLM-assisted Framework to Focus Human Relabeling

Brunello, Andrea; Curaba, Cristian; Geatti, Luca; Mignani, Michele; Montanari, Angelo; Saccomanno, Nicola. First public date recorded: 2026-06-01.

[Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02837) · [Inspected source](<../fulltext/R042.pdf>)

**Source type.** Annotation audit and assistance method

**Purpose.** Identify and repair annotation errors in reasoning datasets.

**Intended reviewers.** Dataset auditors with logic and annotation expertise.

**Concepts and criteria.** Correctness, ambiguity, reference labels and targeted human relabeling.

**Answer format and guidance.** Verification and prioritisation workflow with model assistance.

**Missing information.** Distinguishes flawed formalization and ambiguity; detection is not proof all remaining labels are correct.

**Accessibility and effort.** Reduction in instances requiring review is not measured reading time or novice accessibility.

**Evaluation and findings.** Audits 275 FOLIO validation items and the first 100 MALLS items, with a 213 item control; reports errors and explores selective review.

**Evidence assessment.** Fixed sample and domain constrain generalization. Human labels and formal verification each have assumptions.

**Evidence locator.** Sections 2–4; PDF pp. 2–12; limitations.
