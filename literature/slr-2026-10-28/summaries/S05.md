# S05: A framework for characterising evaluation instruments of AI performance, Chapter 6 in AI and the Future of Skills, Volume 2

Anthony G. Cohn; José Hernández-Orallo. First public date recorded: 2023-11-16.

[Primary record](https://doi.org/10.1787/f585066f-en) · [Inspected source](<../fulltext/S05.pdf>)

**Source type.** Classification framework with application

**Purpose.** Categorise AI evaluation instruments for comparing capabilities. Review unit: Chapter 6 within the volume.

**Intended reviewers.** AI evaluation researchers and instrument selectors; chapter describes student raters.

**Concepts and criteria.** 18 facets under Validity, Consistency and Fairness. These groups address what is measured, how it is measured and which system is measured.

**Answer format and guidance.** Facet specific categorical values, explanations and examples. Some values express preferences; other facets categorise purposes without a universal preferred value.

**Missing information.** Reliability allows N/A when methodology or common use is unclear. This is not identical to an explicit finding of poor reliability or inapplicability.

**Accessibility and effort.** Definitions and guided rating. Raters were AI related undergraduate/graduate students; time taken in hours was requested (printed p. 104). This records effort collection, but the chapter does not report a comparative novice accessibility or time saving result. Agreement is not correctness.

**Evaluation and findings.** 36 instruments; first two rated by coordinators and all raters, next eleven by four raters, then two per instrument. Discussion led to revisions. Harder facets included Capability, Coverage, Specificity, Adjustability and Ambition.

**Evidence assessment.** Application and rater discussion provide evidence of use, not validation for general readers. Do not call the 18 facets a benchmark quality scale.

**Evidence locator.** Chapter 6, printed pp. 99–118, especially pp. 100–110; PDF pp. 101–112. Table 6.1 and facet definitions pp. 101–102.

**Version.** Retrieved edition/version: see source file and version audit. Earlier public versions not fully compared.
