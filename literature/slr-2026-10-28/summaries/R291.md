# R291: What AI Benchmarks Actually Measure: Adapting Convergent and Discriminant Validity to Interrogate Fifty-Six AI Benchmarks

Desai, Meera; Truong, Sang T.; Wallach, Hanna; Chouldechova, Alex; Cooper, A. Feder; Garcia-Gathright, Jean; Ho, Daniel E.; Jacobs, Abigail Z.; Koyejo, Sanmi; Pangakis, Nicholas; Wang, Angelina. First public date recorded: 2026-09-08.

[Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08812) · [Inspected source](<../fulltext/R291.pdf>)

**Source type.** Empirical benchmark validity audit

**Purpose.** Test whether benchmark score patterns support intended constructs.

**Intended reviewers.** Researchers using psychometrics and benchmark results.

**Concepts and criteria.** Convergent/discriminant validity and item response theory.

**Answer format and guidance.** Statistical analysis, not a review questionnaire.

**Missing information.** Unclear construct definitions limit interpretation; correlations alone do not identify causes.

**Accessibility and effort.** No reviewer accessibility intervention.

**Evaluation and findings.** Analyses 56 benchmarks and 53 models; reports weaknesses in convergence and separation of some intended concepts.

**Evidence assessment.** Models, benchmark coverage and general ability confounding constrain interpretation.

**Evidence locator.** Methods/results; discussion and limitations PDF pp. 10–11.
