# B20: Evaluation data contamination in LLMs: how do we measure it and (when) does it matter?

Singh, Aaditya K.; Kocyigit, Muhammed Yusuf; Poulton, Andrew; Esiobu, David; Lomeli, Maria; Szilvasy, Gergely; Hupkes, Dieuwke. First public date recorded: 2024-11-06.

[Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.03923) · [Inspected source](<../fulltext/B20.pdf>)

**Source type.** Empirical contamination measurement method

**Purpose.** Assess contamination metrics by their relation to score inflation.

**Intended reviewers.** AI evaluation researchers; technical knowledge is assumed by the method.

**Concepts and criteria.** ConTAM; metric thresholds, tasks and model effects.

**Answer format and guidance.** Method, definitions or recommendations as described in the evaluation field; no general categorical benchmark review questionnaire.

**Missing information.** No shared missing information response scale. Report unavailable evidence separately from the observed experimental finding.

**Accessibility and effort.** No measured novice benchmark review understanding or effort identified in the inspected method/results. This is not a claim about every possible use of the work.

**Evaluation and findings.** 13 benchmarks and seven models from two families; comparison of contamination measures.

**Evidence assessment.** Thresholds and model size interact; one global overlap cutoff is not sufficient.

**Evidence locator.** Methods 3–4; results 5–6; discussion 8, PDF p. 17.
