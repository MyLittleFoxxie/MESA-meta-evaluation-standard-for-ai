For other arXiv sources, `searches/metadata-*.json` contains the title, authors, initial date and version history from the primary record. For publisher sources, the register retains the DOI and retrieved text or landing page. Some index dates specify only a year; no day is invented. An earliest arXiv submission verifies that record’s history, not the absence of an earlier release elsewhere. Publication only dates and possible unlocated preprints remain an author audit item.
