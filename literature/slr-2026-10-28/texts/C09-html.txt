Approach: PRISM-Bench integrates a pre-registered PRISM-Spec, sealed execution with telemetry, StressLab for invariances and shifts, a severity-weighted error ontology (PRISM-Taxon), hardware budget ladders with device calibrators, and open EvalCards. Results: Within-budget rankings with confidence intervals, quality–efficiency Pareto fronts, and robustness/error summaries are reported using Stress Weighted Robustness Score (SWRS), with content-addressed artifacts ensuring exact reruns across datasets and hardware. Impact: Apples-to-apples comparisons become transparent and repeatable, with hardware effects contextualized rather than normalized away, consistent with recent guidance on standardized, context-aware benchmarking.
