# Multi-Dimensional Related Work Comparison Matrix

This matrix delineates **Paper 2 ("Using AI Benchmark Results as Requirements Evidence")** against nine foundational assurance and requirements frameworks, as well as recent benchmark oracle auditing literature.

| Framework / Work | (1) Input Requirements Format | (2) Evidence Source & Granularity | (3) Applicability & Validity Criteria | (4) Handling of Missing / Unstated Evidence | (5) Decision Outcomes / Verdicts | (6) Implementation Rigor | (7) Empirical Evaluation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **AMLAS** (Hawkins et al., 2021) | High-level system safety requirements allocated to ML components (e.g., hazard avoidance goals). | Internal training, validation, and test datasets generated within the safety lifecycle. | Process-oriented stage gates (data verification, model learning assurance). | Assumes complete internal documentation; silent on external missing evidence. | Assurance argument claims: Validated / Deficient. | Process guidance and Goal Structuring Notation (GSN) patterns; no executable code. | Case studies on autonomous driving / robotics simulations. |
| **Assurance Contracts** (Sljivo et al., 2014; Kelly, 1998) | Component assumptions and guarantees expressed in formal contracts. | Internal component architectural models and test execution artifacts. | Logical satisfaction of assumptions by peer guarantees. | Undefined; assumes contracts are fully populated. | Contract satisfied, broken, or indeterminate. | Formal metamodels and tool-supported argument generation. | Industrial avionic and automotive architectural models. |
| **Safety Case Impact Assessment** (Kokaly et al., 2017) | Safety goals linked to component specification items. | Safety case argument fragments and change logs. | Impact analysis assessing which sub-arguments are invalidated by a change. | Flagged as requiring re-assurance; lacks a calculus for third-party evidence. | Re-assurance required vs. Argument unaffected. | Metamodel and dependency-tracing algorithms. | Case study on safety-critical automotive software modifications. |
| **Transfer Assurance** (Picardi et al., 2023) | Operational domain specifications and transfer requirements. | Retrained model evaluation data and transfer distance metrics. | Bounded distribution shift and similarity between source and target domains. | Requires explicit measurement of source and target operational parameters. | Assurance transfer valid vs. Re-assessment mandated. | Conceptual framework and GSN argument templates. | Autonomous driving perceptual shift scenarios. |
| **VNVSpec** (Arief et al., 2026) | Machine-readable V&V requirement specifications with acceptance metrics. | Internal unit and integration test execution traces (pytest, Jest). | Direct execution of test assertions against requirements specifications. | Failed checks or unmapped tests marked as missing coverage. | Verified, Failed, Incomplete Specification. | Open-source Python specification compiler and test harness. | Internal benchmark and synthetic micro-requirements. |
| **Assurance Evidence Reuse** (Mukhopadhyay & Nalla, 2026) | Declared stage dependencies and policy assertions. | Signed execution traces and DVC dependency hashes across pipeline revisions. | Validity conditions for reusing pipeline stages without re-execution. | Undeclared or changed dependencies force fail-closed stage re-execution. | Evidence Reusable vs. Re-execution Mandated. | Cryptographically signed release gate with Ed25519 signatures. | Replay of 714 public DVC pipelines across revisions. |
| **Assurance Envelopes for Coding Agents** (Goswami, 2026) | Preserved software properties and change obligations. | Accumulated artifacts from prior coding agent runs (tests, type checks, proofs). | Inference graph closure restoring verified properties after software change. | Unclosed obligation triggers complete re-verification. | Valid Envelope Exists vs. Re-verification Required. | Forward-chaining over typed inference graphs + CP-SAT solver. | Benchmark of multi-step software change maintenance obligations. |
| **NIST AI Benchmark Guidance** (NIST AI 800-2, 2026) | General organizational AI risk management and capability goals. | Public or proprietary benchmark leaderboard reports. | General guidelines for transparency, documentation, and error analysis. | Recommends reporting limitations; no formal decision calculus. | Descriptive risk assessment; no automated pass/fail. | Standard publication / guidance document. | None (Policy and metrology recommendations). |
| **DAMSA–SMS** (Singh & Kaewprapha, 2026) | Risk-traceable operational requirements and deployment constraints. | Empirical evaluation metrics across operational hazard test suites and benchmarks. | Pareto optimality over performance, cost, and hazard criteria. | Unverified risk metrics filter candidate models from consideration. | Ranked deployment candidates via DAMSA Pareto screening. | Three-layer decision architecture with Pareto screening and robustness analysis. | Multi-attribute deployment model selection across predictive ML benchmarks. |
| **ECBD** (Liu et al., ACL 2024) | Intended benchmark use specifications and linguistic capability constructs. | NLP benchmark design choices, item pools, and scoring rubrics. | Evidence-centered design worksheet (describe, justify, support). | Flags unjustified design choices as validity threats. | Benchmark Design Qualified vs. Flawed. | 20-question construct validity design worksheet. | Authoring audits across NLP benchmark datasets. |
| **BetterBench** (Reuel et al., NeurIPS 2024) | AI benchmark quality criteria across 15 dimensions. | Benchmark repositories, dataset inspection, implementation code, audits. | Multi-attribute quality scoring (construct validity, test quality, data quality). | Penalizes undocumented criteria with zero scores. | Multi-attribute benchmark quality profile. | Open-source audit scripts and evaluation rubrics. | Empirical audits of 15 major AI benchmarks. |
| **Evaluation Cards** (Ghosh et al., 2026) | Standardized evaluation reporting schemas for diverse stakeholder personas. | Benchmark evaluation metadata, task descriptions, test parameters, limitations. | Interpretive reporting transparency isolating documentation gaps. | Displays documentation gaps visually in reporting cards. | Structured qualitative reporting layer for human readers. | Schema templates and visualization tools. | Interpretive reporting across benchmark evaluation scenarios. |
| **Beyond the Hype** (Pizarro et al., 2026) | Industrial LLM adoption requirements and capability criteria. | Systematic evaluation datasets and industrial adoption benchmarks. | Methodological framework evaluating benchmark saturation, gaming, and limits. | Highlights that unverified pass rates provide illusory evidence for adoption. | Informed adoption decision vs. Deployment risk. | Systematic evaluation methodology for industrial adoption. | Case studies of LLM evaluation in software engineering industry contexts. |
| **Model Selection in SE** (Hou et al., 2024; Zheng et al., 2024) | Task descriptions (e.g., code generation, bug fixing). | Public benchmark rankings (HumanEval, SWE-bench). | Aggregate leaderboard rankings or heuristic capability matching. | Ignores missing evidence; treats missing documentation as irrelevant. | Model chosen (highest score). | Empirical survey / heuristic selection scripts. | Retrospective performance analysis across tasks. |
| **Oracle Auditing** (Ding et al., 2026; EvalPlus) | Natural language docstrings and prompt specifications. | Benchmark hidden test suites and execution traces. | Test suite sensitivity and over-constraint detection (identifying false failures). | Documents unstated assumptions in test oracles. | Oracle Sound vs. Over-constrained. | Test fuzzer and AST comparison scripts. | Audits of HumanEval, MBPP, SWE-bench Lite. |
| **This Work (Paper 2)** | **Software requirements for automated coding assistants on target repositories (e.g., $\ge 70\%$ issue resolution under budget).** | **External public benchmark evidence (SWE-bench Verified) paired with MESA meta-evaluation records.** | **Formal 3-tier admissibility calculus: Wilson confidence bounds, repository-matched tasks, and MESA Kane scoring inference gates.** | **Separates unstated documentation from verified defect; missing required evidence yields Unresolved rather than silent match or rejection.** | **Supported, Contradicted, Unresolved (with explicit reason traces).** | **Executable Python decision engine (`decision_engine.py`) and reproducible test harness.** | **Empirical comparison across decision methods evaluated against ground-truth outcomes on unseen issues in 3 mature Python repositories (`requests`, `flask`, `sympy`).** |
