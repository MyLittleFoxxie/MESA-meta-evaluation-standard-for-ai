# MESA model study: 30 conversations

## Aim and design
MESA is one fixed 25-question questionnaire with explanations, five answer choices, optional comments and no total quality score. Compare reviews using MESA, the original complete EFPA Test Review Model (EFPA-TRM), and general benchmark-review instructions. Requested model gpt-6.1-sol, medium reasoning effort, no assigned reviewer identity. Five fresh conversations per method review ARC-AGI-2 then DesignQA; five other fresh conversations per method assess all 24 constructed cases and 49 claims. Total: 30 conversations, 30 benchmark reviews, 10 MESA questionnaires and 250 MESA choices, and 735 repeated claim answers if all outputs are usable. The 735 are repeated answers to 49 distinct claims, not independent observations. No role-group comparison, human simulation, or second-model confirmation.

## Change of design and provenance
The user selected this smaller design after the larger 180-conversation series began. That series was stopped from scheduling new jobs, active jobs were allowed to finish, and all its records remain archived separately. No earlier outputs are reused or pooled here. All 30 conversations are fresh. The reason for the change is the requested execution budget, not a selection based on which outputs were favorable. Inputs and analysis for this design are frozen before its first recorded run. This is a locally recorded protocol, not public preregistration. Case texts, expectations and review criteria existed before this study and have already been used; they are not independently held-out tests. The same assistant prepared the questionnaire, cases, expected answers and analysis.

## Materials and outputs
All MESA conversations receive byte-identical instrument text copied from the latest questionnaire explanations. Questions, IDs, six domains, five choices and HLE example are unchanged. The live survey remains unchanged. Each method receives identical benchmark passages, intended uses, test cases, neutral instructions and common output requirements. MESA alone receives its explanations and HLE example; EFPA-TRM receives the full extracted 90-page original model with forms and guidance, with the PDF authoritative. Amounts of guidance and method-specific tasks differ. EFPA-TRM requests every numbered field/item, with explicit IDs and shared reasons for grouped items, missing information, inapplicability and questions needing a different meaning for AI. It is not the answer key.

The common benchmark conclusion separates claims justified by the passage, problems explicitly reported, and information needed but not supplied. Review bodies are strings and common lists contain strings. MESA requires 25 answers per benchmark; comments may be absent, empty or null, or text. No extra required reason or second review pass. The 24 cases appear in a fixed order, the first eight followed by the sixteen paired cases. Each claim is classified Supported, Contradicted or Not established with an exact excerpt and reason using the assigned method. A full instrument review per constructed case is not required. Expected answers and reasons are analyst-only and never supplied to reviewers.

## Outcomes and fixed analysis
Eligibility requires completed execution and turn, valid JSON with unique keys and correct field types, complete expected case/claim or questionnaire IDs exactly once, and no observed reviewer tool use. Preserve completed invalid responses without repair or replacement. Report planned, attempted, completed, usable and excluded denominators per batch and method. Count expected-answer matches per conversation and case, and 23 prespecified related-claim comparisons per conversation (345 planned). For predefined negative claims in the sixteen-case portion, report Supported answers where the key does not support the claim (90 planned opportunities); these are limited claims, not a review-prose error rate. Quote substring checks locate text, not semantic correctness.

Report MESA choice distributions and variation over 50 question-benchmark pairs across five repetitions. Ten dependent answer pairs per question-benchmark pair give 500 planned repeated-choice comparisons if all five outputs are valid. Choices are categories, never numerical quality scores. Repetition, claims and paired comparisons are not independent; no significance tests, power or equivalence claim. Equal, worse and better outcomes must all be reported.

The six illustrative reviews are fixed as neutral repetition 1 for each method, each covering both benchmarks. Inspect complete outputs against the twelve frozen passage-specific points. Do not replace selected invalid responses; read them only as a separately labeled illustration, outside structured counts. Record exact excerpts, information retained or left unaddressed, conclusions needing qualification, and EFPA-TRM reinterpretations. Distinguish an omitted questionnaire requirement from an absent detail in one response. This is interpretation by the assistant conducting the study, not independent correctness or an error/omission rate. Read all usable MESA real closing notes for reported ambiguity without treating them as human comprehension or hidden model reasoning.

## Isolation, execution and failures
Fresh ephemeral CLI conversations, empty working directories, no inherited history, project documents or memories, disabled external search, shell and multi-agent tools, read-only sandbox and explicit prohibition on external information. Save exact inputs, settings, fingerprints, dates, commands, raw events, response text, stderr and failures. Pretrained knowledge and platform instructions remain possible influences. Backend revision, temperature and generation seed are unavailable. The seed orders jobs only. At most two concurrent reviewer processes. Stop scheduling on infrastructure failure. One retained retry is allowed only after an execution failure without any completed response or turn. Completed invalid responses are never retried, repaired or replaced.

## Reporting and preservation
Only this 30-conversation series supplies the paper's new model results. Earlier and stopped series are archived unchanged and not pooled. The paper calls the latest questionnaire MESA; historical study labels remain only in archives. Easier human use is a design goal, not an observed benefit. Human interpretation, effort and decision effects are future work for CHASE and intended evaluation for REFSQ. Preserve bibliography, other manuscripts, survey, earlier inputs, expectations, outputs, analyses and agent profiles. Do not compile LaTeX or open PDF preview; layout and page length remain unchecked.
