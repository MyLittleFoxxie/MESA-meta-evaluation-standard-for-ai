This makes the score transparent and reproducible for the inspected software, and public tests cover several error types, though the preparer did not independently test the whole marking system. Test construction has real strengths: a large human study (407 people, 515 sessions) informed selection, the three 120-problem test sets were matched for human difficulty, and the creators tried to reduce brute-force program search and repeated rules. Limits: the human study pool was larger than the final set, so results do not all describe the final tests. Matching for human difficulty does not address AI score variability.
