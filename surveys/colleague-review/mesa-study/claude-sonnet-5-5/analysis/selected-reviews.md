### mesa — designqa

No licence file or complete reuse rules found.

Question 23 is answered No (and question 24 No) although the passage says these findings describe limits of the materials inspected and do not establish that permission is unavailable. Not sure would follow MESA's rule not to convert missing information into No. The same response's closing note says missing information was marked Not sure.

### mesa — arc

Verified policy uses one run; no repeat estimates.

Question 14 is answered No (four of five MESA repetitions; one chose Not sure). The verified-testing policy does establish one official run, but the passage does not rule out repeat-run studies by others, so the answer depends on which procedure is being reviewed.

### ordinary — arc

Training on development problems is allowed.

Listed under documented problems, although the passage presents this as the stated policy that keeps development problems separate from evaluation problems; it is not shown to be a defect.

### ordinary — arc

No estimates of score variation when the same AI repeats the test.

Missing information is listed as a documented problem; the passage reports no such estimates but does not show that scores vary.

### efpa — arc

No study shows scores predict success in language, social interaction or real-world work.

Listed under documented problems, while the same review's supported claims call these broader links unresolved rather than disproved.
