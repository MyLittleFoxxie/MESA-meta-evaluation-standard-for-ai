## Conclusions needing qualification

- MESA / ARC-AGI-2: Q14 is No with the comment “Verified policy states a single run, and no variation estimates are provided.” This establishes the official policy, not the absence of other repeat-run studies. All five MESA responses choose No here.
- MESA / ARC-AGI-2: Q12 says “Public tests cover some cases, but the whole system was not independently tested.” The passage says that the preparer did not independently test the whole system; it does not establish that nobody did.
- MESA / DesignQA: “Reviewer disagreement rates and remaining errors are not reported.” appears under documented problems, while corresponding counts also appear as missing information. Lack of these counts is not evidence that the answers are wrong.
- MESA / DesignQA: Q24 is Partly because code history identifies changes, but the same response lists a clear record connecting changes to scores as missing. This distinguishes partial documentation from complete comparison support.
- General instructions / ARC-AGI-2: permitted training on development problems is placed under documented problems, although the passage states a policy without showing it is defective. Missing repeat-run estimates also appear there.
- EFPA-TRM / ARC-AGI-2: absence of predictive studies is listed as a documented problem while broader links are also called unresolved. Missing evidence and a demonstrated lack of relationship remain different conclusions.

No inference about human behavior follows from these model examples. A conditional shortcut statement need not include a second disclaimer to avoid asserting actual exploitation.
