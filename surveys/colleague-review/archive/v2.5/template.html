What you are being asked to do

__MESA_INTRODUCTION__

You are reviewing the tests, not solving their problems or rating a particular AI. You will answer the same 25 questions for each of two benchmarks, making 50 benchmark selections in total. The final review page also asks five required feedback questions; “Cannot judge” is available, and written feedback is optional. Read the supplied passages and use them to make your own judgments. You may return to them while answering.

There is no time limit. Comments are optional, and you do not need to supply evidence or citations. The completed HLE example explains how one reviewer used the questionnaire; your own answers may differ.

Words with a circled question mark have a short definition. Hover over them, focus them with your keyboard, or tap or click to read it.
