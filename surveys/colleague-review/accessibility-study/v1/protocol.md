# MESA accessibility-guidance study, version 1

## Purpose and boundaries
MESA remains 25 questions, six domains and five categorical choices, with optional comments and no aggregate quality score. The proposed guidance aims to make the questions easier to interpret without adding required answers or explanations. Human ease of use, review effort and decision benefits are not measured. This study evaluates model-generated reviews and answers to constructed claims, not humans or benchmark execution. The earlier Sol and Astra series remain unchanged and are not pooled with this study.

## Conditions and design
Original MESA, revised MESA, original EFPA-TRM, and general review instructions. One requested model: gpt-6.1-sol, medium reasoning effort. Neutral instructions only, five separate repetitions per method in each batch: 20 published-benchmark conversations and 20 separate test-case conversations, 40 planned in total. Each published-benchmark conversation reviews ARC-AGI-2 then DesignQA: 40 planned benchmark reviews. The two MESA conditions together yield 20 questionnaires and 500 choices. Each test-case conversation answers 32 claims in 16 cases: 640 planned repeated answers, not independent observations. Repeated runs represent one model, not people. No second-model confirmation is planned here.

Both MESA methods receive the same exact questions, choices, definitions, passages and HLE examples. Only question help, optional comment prompts and closing reminders differ. The complete added package is tested; effects of individual additions cannot be isolated. Model conversations receive help as text; this does not test expandable HTML or whether humans would open help. Each condition receives the same benchmark evidence, intended uses and common output requirements. Full EFPA text includes the original 90-page structure and guidance; extraction is not a substitute for the authoritative PDF. Condition-specific outputs differ appropriately, but common conclusions and claim-classification outputs are shared.

## Chronology and freezing
The pre-change master baseline is 031437277cb0860aedc3a6bf8a353d8ac54e18f2. Guidance and its text fingerprints were frozen in guidance-freeze.json before these assessment cases were written. Case expectations, comparison checklist, protocol, prompts and inputs are then frozen in freeze-manifest.json before recorded execution. This is a local freeze, not public preregistration, independent case construction or independent validation. Same assistant prepares guidance, cases, expected answers, scripts and interpretations. Expected answers and organizer records are never supplied to reviewer conversations. Any post-freeze material change requires a new version; do not overwrite frozen inputs.

## Task and expected outcomes
Eight paired families cover missing versus explicit negative information, favorable versus unfavorable repeated results, unrelated additions, examination of supplied answers, marking-software tests, narrow versus broad intended uses, scoring requirements, and repeated versus changed conditions. Each case has two claims. Analyst-only expected.json records labels, reasons and expected pairs before testing. These cases deliberately reuse conceptual distinctions from earlier development examples but use new passages. They are not independent held-out evidence of general review correctness.

Primary descriptive outcome: matches with the predefined answers per conversation and family, comparing revised against original MESA. Also report EFPA-TRM and general instructions. Report incorrect Supported selections for predefined negative-conclusion claims where the expected answer is not Supported; this is a mechanically defined false-negative-conclusion indicator limited to those claims, not a rate for all review prose. For each paired family report whether both case answers match the expected pair, and separately whether answers should change or remain unchanged. Do not treat every claim, pair or repetition as independent. No significance tests, power claim, or aggregate benchmark-quality score. Equal, worse and better outcomes are all reported; no selective expansion after a ceiling result.

For published benchmarks, report all five MESA choice distributions and exact matches between each pair of five repetitions (10 pairs per question and benchmark; 500 dependent pairs per MESA method). Matching choices do not establish correctness. Eight illustrative reviews are fixed as repetition 1 for each method and both benchmarks; compare complete output against the twelve-point checklist frozen in analyst/real-comparison-checklist.json. No replacement if a selected response is invalid. The assistant records passage references, quotations, whether a point is addressed, and qualifications. No independent human correctness assessment is claimed.

## Isolation, settings and execution
Reuse the earlier runner's fresh ephemeral CLI sessions, empty working directories, no inherited conversation, project documents or memories, disabled web search and shell/multi-agent tools, read-only sandbox and explicit prohibition of external reading. Save commands and observed tool events; configuration cannot remove pretrained knowledge or platform instructions. Backend revision, temperature and generation seed are unavailable unless genuinely exposed. A scheduling seed orders jobs only, not model generation. Use at most three concurrent processes and stop scheduling on infrastructure failure. Execution time and tokens describe infrastructure, not human review effort.

## Failures and eligibility
Retain every raw attempt, event log, stderr, available response, timestamps, settings and hashes. Allow one separately retained retry only for an execution failure without a completed turn or response; no third attempt. Never repair, replace or retry a completed malformed response. A response is usable for structured analysis only when the turn completed, JSON and task schema are valid, all required cases/questions/claims are present exactly once, and no reviewer tool use was observed. MESA comments may be absent, empty or null. Lists and common conclusions must have the specified types. Empty conclusions are valid and may be noted during illustration. Quote-substring checks establish location only, not semantic correctness. Report planned, attempted, completed, valid and excluded denominators by batch and method. Preserve invalid records; inspect them separately without silently incorporating repaired values. Do not interpret service failures as wrong answers. If access fails repeatedly, report the limitation and unperformed jobs rather than changing the requested model or inventing outputs.
