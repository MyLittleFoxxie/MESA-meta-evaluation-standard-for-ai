# Qualitative comparison plan for real passages

Prepared 14 October 2026 after three runs were available. Those were EFPA/benchmark-reviewer/repetition 4 and ordinary/newcomer/repetition 2 and ordinary/benchmark-reviewer/repetition 3. This plan is not preregistration. It fixes a limited descriptive sample before examining the selected outputs: repetition 1 in the neutral role, all three conditions, both benchmarks (six reviews). No outcome-based replacement is permitted if a selected run fails.

Inspect native review and common conclusions separately. A concern missing from a common conclusion may still appear in the native review. Record exact output quotations and run IDs. Mark an issue addressed, qualified, absent in the inspected field, or inconsistent with the supplied passage; do not turn these tags into a validated review-quality score. Neither EFPA nor any real-case response is an answer key.

## ARC-AGI-2 passage concerns

1. Bounded interpretation: performance on grid tasks versus broader intelligence or real-world transfer.
2. Exact grid scoring versus averaging successes within multi-input tasks; not conflating a partial grid with a partially solved task.
3. Human difficulty matching is not repeated-run AI stability; no supplied repeat variation estimates.
4. Public data exposure and hidden-set safeguards do not establish a model's actual training exposure.
5. Public runnable materials versus controlled hidden sets; version changes and absent before/after score comparisons.
6. Human comparisons and differences across evaluation routes require stated conditions; no claim that human-background findings establish universal fairness.

## DesignQA passage concerns

1. Narrow single-rulebook tasks versus untested broad engineering competence or other standards.
2. Documented wording/formatting and reference-coverage problems, distinct from merely missing scoring-validation evidence.
3. Unequal category sizes and equal-weight aggregation; no demonstrated distinct ability structure.
4. Changed passage-set tests are not repeated identical-condition testing; small performance group and uncertain close rankings.
5. Always-yes shortcut opportunity in compliant examples, without claiming any model actually exploited it; exposure uncertainty distinct from proven contamination.
6. Partial replication records and missing licensing/change documentation, without asserting legal prohibition or total unavailability.
