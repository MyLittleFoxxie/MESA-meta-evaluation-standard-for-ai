# Exploratory examination of MESA closing notes

Prepared 15 October 2026 after 43 Sol sessions had become eligible (450 MESA choices and 18 available benchmark closing notes). This is a post-observation descriptive analysis plan, not a preregistered hypothesis or independent validation.

Read all closing notes from the completed Sol MESA sessions, preserving run and benchmark IDs. Each benchmark note is one generated text, not a person or an independent human observation. Report exact quotations and counts with a denominator of available notes where counts are used. Do not infer lack of difficulty from an empty note.

Separate at least these issues:

- Questions about whether checks occurred versus how extensive they were; what counts as full or partial support.
- Scope of the reviewed entity, including public/hidden tasks, verified/community evaluation, and AI alone versus the whole information-supply system.
- Distinctions between repeated identical runs and changed inputs, scoring rules and broader interpretation.
- Problems introduced by the experiment's own instructions, including its unexplained phrase “condition-native review.” This phrase is absent from the canonical questionnaire. Such comments must not be attributed to MESA's participant-facing wording.

The phrase above is retained in the frozen executed prompts. Do not silently replace it after observing feedback. Future protocol versions should say “a review using the assigned method.” A clearer explanation in the manuscript or supplementary reading copy does not change which text the recorded models received. The output-format issue is a limitation of this experiment, not evidence that human reviewers found the questionnaire confusing.

This guide supports an exploratory reading of generated comments. It does not establish why a model selected a category, whether the difficulty is experienced by people, or a validated classification of all possible interpretation problems.
