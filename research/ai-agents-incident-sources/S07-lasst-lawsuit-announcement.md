## Summary (factual description)

Announcement of a lawsuit against OpenAI. LASST argues that the hack violates California's computer-crime law (CDAFA, Cal. Penal Code § 502(c)) and that California law (Cal. Civ. Code § 1714.46(b)) does not allow 'the AI did it autonomously' as a defence. It frames the suit as holding frontier developers accountable for unsafe agent behaviour.
