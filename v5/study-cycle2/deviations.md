# Study Cycle 2 Protocol Deviations and Post-Results Audit Record

This record documents deviations from the frozen Study Cycle 2 protocol, methodological issues identified during analysis review, and read-only supplemental audits added after Cycle 2 execution. No frozen input, model response, manifest, or analysis output in `v4/`, `v5/study/`, or `v5/study-cycle2/freeze.py` (`FROZEN_FILES`) was altered.

## 1. Blind Author Rating Check: Not Carried Out

- **Decision:** The planned blind author check in `v5/study-cycle2/blind_author_sheet.json` was not executed before manuscript submission. `blind_author_sheet.json` remains untouched with empty `author_label` values (`0/72` in `disagreements_72`, `0/62` in `part_b_62`, `0/64` in `part_a_64`), and `v5/study-cycle2/reveal_blind.py` was not run.
- **Reason:** Insufficient author time before the submission deadline to independently rate 198 items without unblinding or rushing.
- **Sample exposure:** During repository inspection prior to this decision (`2026-11-11`), `v5/study-cycle2/blind_author_key.json` may have been viewed. Any future human-rating check must therefore draw a fresh stratified sample or use an independent human rater who has not seen the key or model outputs.
- **Three defects in the built blind check (to fix before any future reuse):**
  1. *Missing label options on disagreements:* `disagreements_72` in `blind_author_sheet.json` restricts `allowed_causes` to three options (`missing_rule`, `granularity`, `reading`), whereas `blind_author_key.json` holds five causes (`granularity`: 22, `missing_rule`: 21, `reading`: 20, `threshold`: 8, `sources`: 1), leaving 9 items with no valid selectable option on the sheet.
  2. *Duplicate benchmark-facet rows treated as independent:* `part_a_64` contains 64 rows representing only 32 unique `(benchmark, facet)` properties (4 benchmarks $\times$ 8 facets, duplicated across repeats 1 and 2), while `reveal_blind.py` passes all 64 pairs to a flat row-level bootstrap that treats dependent repeats as independent observations.
  3. *Facet prefixing inflates Cohen's $\kappa$:* `reveal_blind.py` (lines 93–101) prefixes every Part A category with its facet name (`f"{item['facet']}:{category}"`) before computing one pooled Cohen's $\kappa$ across all rows. Combining eight distinct facet category sets under facet prefixes artificially reduces chance agreement $p_e$ and inflates $\kappa$.

## 2. Part A Pooled Nominal Krippendorff's $\alpha$ Omitted

- **Issue found:** Both `v5/study/reliability.py` and `v5/study-cycle2/reliability.py` (line 147) prefix each Part A category with its facet (`f + ":" + part_a_category(d[f])`) and pool all 8 facets (which each have their own distinct category sets) into a single nominal Krippendorff's $\alpha$ computation. This produces three pooled nominal $\alpha$ values in frozen summaries (`v5/study/analysis/summary.json` and `v5/study-cycle2/analysis/summary.json`):
  - Within Claude: `part_a_alpha_nominal = 0.797` (95% CI `[0.627, 0.930]`)
  - Within Gemini: `part_a_alpha_nominal = 0.931` (95% CI `[0.824, 1.000]`)
  - Cross-family 4-rater pooled Part A: `alpha_4_raters = 0.806` (95% CI `[0.695, 0.898]`)
- **Action taken:** None of those three pooled Part A $\alpha$ values is reported in `mesa_paper.tex`. Instead, the manuscript reports the per-facet raw agreement counts already computed in `v5/study-cycle2/analysis/summary.json`:
  - **Within Claude (32 pairs; 26/32 category, 23/32 text):** Purpose `4/4` (`4/4` text), Judgeability `4/4` (`4/4`), Autonomy `4/4` (`3/4`), Reference `3/4` (`3/4`), Objectivity `3/4` (`3/4`), Progression `3/4` (`3/4`), Variation `3/4` (`3/4`), Containedness `2/4` (`0/4`).
  - **Within Gemini (32 pairs; 30/32 category, 29/32 text):** Purpose `4/4` (`4/4`), Judgeability `4/4` (`4/4`), Reference `4/4` (`4/4`), Autonomy `4/4` (`4/4`), Progression `4/4` (`4/4`), Variation `4/4` (`4/4`), Objectivity `3/4` (`3/4`), Containedness `3/4` (`2/4`).
  - **Cross-family (128 cross pairs across 4 raters, 16 cross pairs per facet; 102/128 total):** Purpose `16/16`, Judgeability `16/16`, Reference `14/16`, Progression `14/16`, Objectivity `12/16`, Autonomy `12/16`, Variation `10/16`, Containedness `8/16`.
- **Scope note:** Part B ratings share a single 6-level scale across criteria (and 5 ordered levels when `Not applicable` is separated), and Tier 2 comparisons share a single 3-verdict scale; Part B $\alpha$ and Tier 2 $\kappa$ values do not mix distinct alphabets and are retained.

## 3. Symmetric Inspection and Override of `known_defects` (`TB-1` False Positive)

- **Frozen matcher output (`v5/study-cycle2/analysis/summary.json`, `known-defects-results.csv`):** Claude `6 / 6 / 6` (Repeat 1 / Repeat 2 / Either out of 7); Gemini `3 / 3 / 4`.
- **Symmetric audit of all 28 `(family, defect_id, repeat)` pairs (20 automated hits and 8 automated misses):**
  - **Gemini `TB-1` override (`True -> False` in Repeats 1 and 2):** In `v5/study-cycle2/known_defects.json`, `TB-1` targets empty ground-truth `actions=[]` and `outputs=[]` in `tau_bench/envs/airline/tasks_test.py` and `retail/tasks_test.py`, allowing a no-op agent to receive reward `1.0`. Its automated matcher defined `locator_substrings = ["tasks_test.py", "base.py", "actions", "outputs"]` on `B05` or `B07`. Both Gemini repeats (`tau-bench`, `B07`, `contradicted/failed`) matched because they cited `tau_bench/envs/base.py` and `"actions"` (`r_actions=data_hash == gt_data_hash`). Manual inspection shows Gemini was reporting a **different** real scoring defect: `calculate_reward` only compares final database hashes and output substrings, accepting policy-violating trajectories (such as acting without required user confirmation or authentication). Neither Gemini repeat checked `tasks_test.py` or identified the empty-task no-op defect (`TB-1`). The automated `TB-1` match for Gemini is therefore overridden to `False`, reducing Gemini's recall on pre-registered known defects from `3 / 3 / 4` to **`2 / 2 / 3`** (`3 of 7` across repeats). `mesa_paper.tex` reports `3 of 7` (`2 / 2 / 3`) and notes that the automated matcher counted a fourth defect that on inspection was a different defect.
  - **Remaining Gemini pairs:** `TB-2` (Repeats 1 and 2) genuinely identifies fixed task instruction ambiguity errors and the repository notice that snapshot tasks are outdated; `TB-3` (Repeat 2) genuinely identifies Checklist `3(c)` claiming error bars absent from `Table 2`; `TQA-2` (Repeat 1) genuinely cites `GPT-judge` validation accuracy (`90–96%`) and `Table 3` error modes on long or qualified answers. On `TQA-2` Repeat 2, Gemini also described GPT-judge failure modes on multi-sentence answers (`[PDF page 20]`), which `analyze.py` scored `False` only because the exact substrings `"Table 3"`, `"4.4"`, `"error"`, and `"89.5"` were absent; that frozen `False` is retained. `HE-1`, `TQA-1`, and `HLE-1` were missed in both repeats, whereas Gemini uniquely flagged the `B07` policy-bypass defect (Repeats 1 and 2) and `B28b` ground-truth task exposure in `Env.reset` (Repeat 1), both verified by `executed-confirmations.json`.
  - **Claude pairs (symmetric check of all 14 rows):** All 12 positive matcher hits (`HE-1`, `TB-2`, `TB-3`, `TQA-1`, `TQA-2`, and `HLE-1` in both repeats) cite and describe the target defect accurately (`HE-1` unisolated `exec()` in `execution.py` line 50; `TB-2` paper Section 5.2 and outdated-task README warning; `TB-3` Checklist `3(c)` vs. Table 2; `TQA-1` `6–7%` disagreement and un-revalidated edits; `TQA-2` `89.5%` human-baseline accuracy and long-answer failure modes; `HLE-1` `15.4%`/`18%` Appendix B audit disagreement). Both `TB-1` repeats missed empty `actions`/`outputs` tasks. Zero Claude matches are false positives, leaving Claude at **`6 / 6 / 6`**.
