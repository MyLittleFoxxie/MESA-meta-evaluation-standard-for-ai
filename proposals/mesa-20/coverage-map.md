# Coverage map: current MESA to MESA-20

2026-09-29. A design mapping, not proof of equal measurement quality. Every current numbered Part A unit and rated Part B item has one primary destination below. Related facts can be cited elsewhere. **A destination means a place to discuss the issue; it does not mean every original check or rating is retained.**

Actions: **Combine** reduces separate fields or judgments; **Conditional detail** omits the routine detailed inventory and investigates it only when consequential; **Remove rating** replaces a separate summary rating with reasoning in Q20. Ordinary combined questions may also need expansion under the guide.

Part A facts assigned to Q14/Q17/Q18/Q19 are recorded in the evidence portion before judgment. The short form preserves the fact/judgment distinction but no longer has a separate Part A field for each such fact.

## EFPA functions: what survives and what changes

| EFPA source | Destination | Adaptation and loss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Part A §§1-5 and General Description | Q1-Q4; source record; evidence notes | Identify the AI benchmark, tasks, scorer and conditions rather than a human test and its administration. Many separate descriptive fields disappear. |
| §6 rationale, development and instructions | Q5-Q6, Q17, Q19 | Preserve the argument and construction evidence; separate documentation and method ratings become one note that must distinguish the two. |
| §7 materials | Q3, Q9, Q16-Q17 | Replace paper booklets with usable data, interfaces, code and controlled access. Human-operator accessibility remains relevant. Separate paper/software ratings disappear. |
| §8 norms and criterion interpretation | Q10, Q12, Q16 | Use claim-relevant humans, model groups, chance and decision boundaries. Human norm-table sampling rules and fixed numerical thresholds are not transferred automatically. |
| §9 reliability/precision | Q9, Q11-Q12 | Preserve relevant repeatability, scorer agreement, form equivalence and uncertainty. Do not equate uncertainty intervals with internal consistency. Internal consistency/IRT methods need targeted examination if the score model relies on them. |
| §10 validity | Q5, Q7-Q8, Q13-Q15, Q20 | Preserve content, internal structure, external relations and limits on use. Human correlation/sample-size anchors are not universal AI thresholds. |
| §11 fair use | Q7, Q16-Q17, Q19 | Adapt to intended system/context comparisons while retaining relevant human group impacts; detailed subgroup checks are conditional. |
| §12 generated reports | Q19 | Extend to papers and leaderboards; keep consequential differences among reports, but lose routine per-report ratings. |
| Final Evaluation and rating guidance | Q20; guide | Preserve a reasoned recommendation and critical-gap rule. Collapse 2-4, retain 0/1/n/a distinctions; remove numeric section summaries. |

This is a function-level EFPA map, not a new row-by-row transcription of EFPA. The current MESA map above is item-level. The older EFPA row map in feedback/95-mesa-core-plan.md remains a historical proposal; its projected item counts are not treated as current counts.
