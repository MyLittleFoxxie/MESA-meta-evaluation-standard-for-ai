# MESA-25: revised short-form proposal

Draft 0.2, 2026-10-01. This revision responds to the request for **direct questions, domains, five descriptive questions and twenty evaluative questions**.

Start with the [questionnaire](questionnaire.md). Definitions and interpretation rules are in the [guide](guide.md). The [mapping](coverage-map.md) accounts for the existing MESA items, and the [checks](checks.md) distinguish this revision's checks from the previous draft's work.

## Plain-language revision

The Survey now defines its basic terms before the questions and gives a short explanation with each question. Technical wording has been replaced where possible, including task split, scoring implementation, score sensitivity, shortcuts and test-specific tuning. These edits have not been tested with new readers. The 25-question structure and source mapping are retained.

## What changed

The questions now stand on their own. There are no “look for,” “check,” or “describe” instructions beneath individual questions. The presentation follows the direct checklist style of Protogerou and Hagger's **final Q-SSP items, Table 4**, shown in the corrected attachment. Its domain grouping informs the layout; its survey-specific content and scoring are not copied.

| Domain | Questions | Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Description | Q1-Q5 | Five factual answers |
| Purpose and development | Q6-Q9 | Four judgments |
| Scoring and comparisons | Q10-Q13 | Four judgments |
| Consistency of results | Q14-Q16 | Three judgments |
| Meaning and fairness | Q17-Q21 | Five judgments |
| Use and reporting | Q22-Q25 | Four judgments |

Compared with draft 0.1, task conditions have their own descriptive question. Task selection and task checking, metric meaning and score aggregation, repetition and sensitivity, exposure and tuning, and reproduction and responsible access are now distinct judgments. The checklist retains twenty questions about methods and claims after the five description questions.

## Checklist answers

All 25 questions use Yes / Partly / No / Not sure / Not applicable. The five description questions ask whether descriptions are provided, rather than requesting written descriptions. No evidence notes, citations or closing summary are required. These options replace the earlier Supported / Weakness / Unknown scheme and do not map to EFPA ratings. No total score is calculated.

The existing source mapping is a topic-coverage record, not proof that this checklist retains every detail of a full review. Its references to evidence notes and a conclusion describe the earlier review format; those are not respondent requirements here. Original templates and benchmark reviews are unchanged.

## Limits

This is an experimental form. Direct wording does not establish agreement, accuracy or completion time. Some questions still group related evidence, notably scorer types (Q12), reference types (Q13), context fairness (Q21) and reproduction materials (Q22). Their significant differences belong in the evidence notes, with fuller analysis where needed.

The earlier [human testing plan](../mesa-20/validation-plan.md) applies in principle, but future tests must name and freeze this 25-question version. Its proposed comparison becomes MESA-25 versus the 40-question scorecard and full template. Earlier reading checks are not treated as independent validation of this revision.

## Completed DesignQA example

The [filled checklist](designqa-example.md) supplies all 25 answers with detailed reviewer comments and a source inventory. The [comparison](designqa-comparison.md) checks each item against all three original MESA reviews, preserves disagreements, and explains why source agreement is not questionnaire validation. It is retained as a provisional, source-checked worked example. These detailed comments do not become requirements for ordinary survey respondents.
